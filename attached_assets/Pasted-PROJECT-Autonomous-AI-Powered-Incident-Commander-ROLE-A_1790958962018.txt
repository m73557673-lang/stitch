STEP 6 — AI INVESTIGATION WORKFLOW:

1. Create or update the incident record.
2. Collect available logs, metrics, health-check results, deployment information, and timestamps.
3. Retrieve relevant runbooks and historical incidents when available.
4. Ask the investigation agent to summarize the evidence.
5. Ask the root-cause agent to produce possible causes and supporting evidence.
6. Calculate or clearly label confidence estimates without presenting unsupported precision.
7. Generate a remediation recommendation and risk assessment.
8. Store the investigation, evidence references, recommendations, and execution status.
9. Display progress in the existing dashboard.
10. Generate a structured post-incident report when appropriate.

Use structured JSON responses where supported, validate model outputs, handle timeouts and rate limits, and avoid duplicate concurrent investigations.

AI conclusions must be grounded in available evidence. Clearly identify missing data and uncertainty. Never treat an AI hypothesis as a confirmed root cause without sufficient validation.
